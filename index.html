How to Play

Each level is a slope field — a map of the differential equation. Every tiny line shows the direction a solution flows at that point. Your job: diagnose it, solve it, then trace it.

1

DIAGNOSE

Classify the equation. Careful — some equations belong to multiple families, and every valid answer counts!

2

SOLVE

Choose the correct next move at every step. Wrong moves drain your Focus — read the feedback, it's the fastest way to learn. Stuck? The 💡 Nudge removes one wrong option for a little Focus.

3

TRACE

Drag from the pulsing dot and follow the arrows until you cross the gold goal line (or reach the gold ring, in the phase plains). You're sketching the real solution curve! Arrow keys work too, and the 📐 Euler overlay shows the classic numerical approximation.

4

REVEAL

See the true curve versus your trace — plus the whole solution family, and a y-vs-t replay on phase-plane fields. Stars come from Focus kept + trace accuracy. Stuck? Peek — but it costs a star.

Later chapters hide the equation (read the field!), break uniqueness, and enter the phase plane — two variables dancing together. Phase-plane fields can overlay their nullclines. Mixed Review on the map interleaves everything you've seen (and resurfaces fields you haven't touched in a while), and the 🔎 Field Quiz flips the game around: match equations to their fields.
